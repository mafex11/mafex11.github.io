Privacy

Yuki is built so that your day stays on your PC. This page says what that means, in plain words.

What stays on your PC

  • Memory. Everything Yuki remembers: chats, pages, calls, people, promises. It’s encrypted with a key only your PC has.
  • Reading the screen. Yuki reads the window in front as text, on your PC. No screenshots, no OCR.
  • Voice and call recordings. Both sides of a call are kept on your PC, never uploaded.
  • Your AI keys. Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter keys are stored on your PC. With Ollama there is no key at all.

What we keep

  • Your email, so you can sign in.
  • The link to your PC: its name, when you linked it, and when it was last online.
  • Tasks you queue while your PC is off, only if you choose Queue. Each one is sealed so only your PC can open it. We can’t read it. It’s deleted when it runs, when you cancel it, or after 24 hours.

What passes through us, sealed

When you use Yuki in a browser, your PC streams what the page needs through our relay. The stream is end-to-end encrypted between your PC and your browser. We pass it on. We can’t read it, and we don’t store it.

Deleting things

Forget any memory from the app or the web; it’s removed on your PC. Delete your account and we remove your email and PC links. Your PC keeps its own memory until you delete it there.
