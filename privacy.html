Privacy

Yuki is built so that your day stays on your PC. Here is what that means, in plain words.

What stays on your PC

  • Memory. Everything Yuki remembers: chats, pages, calls, people, promises. It’s encrypted with a key only your PC has.
  • Reading the screen. Yuki reads the window in front as text, on your PC. No screenshots, no OCR.
  • Calls. What was said on both sides is kept on your PC, never uploaded.
  • Your AI keys. Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter keys stay on your PC. With a cloud model, only what a question needs is sent to that provider. With Ollama there is no key, and nothing leaves.

What Yuki learns about you

What Yuki learns about you stays on your PC, encrypted. Record how I work is off until you turn it on, and it never records typing, the clipboard or the screen.

What we keep

  • Your email, so you can sign in.
  • The link to your PC: its name, when you linked it, and when it was last online.
  • Tasks you queue while your PC is off, only if you choose Queue. Each one is sealed so only your PC can open it. It’s deleted when it runs, when you cancel it, or after 24 hours.

What passes through us, sealed

In a browser, your PC streams what the page needs through our relay, encrypted end to end. We pass it on. We can’t read it, and we don’t store it. See the status page if the relay is slow.

Deleting things

Forget any memory from the app or the web; it’s removed on your PC. Delete your account and we remove your email and PC links. Your PC keeps its own memory until you delete it there.
