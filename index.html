Module 01  /  Sees

It reads your screen. It never takes a picture of it.

Windows describes every app for screen readers: its buttons, lists and text. Yuki reads that description of the window in front. Nothing is photographed. Nothing is guessed from pixels.

Module 05  /  Your PC is the brain

Your memory lives on your PC. The web is a window onto it.

When you open Yuki in a browser, your PC streams what you need through an encrypted relay, live, while it’s on. Ask from the web and the task runs on your PC. We keep your account, the link to your PC and, sealed, any task you queue while it’s off. Nothing else.
