Effects

Every hover effect on the site, once each. Try each one with a mouse, then with Tab, then on a phone. One rule: big panels lock their crop marks, single items light their border.

Mint is only for live things. Everything here also works with the keyboard, by touch, and with motion turned off. All names are invented.
